How the math works

Average weekly earnings is taken from the 8 weeks before the qualifying week. For an evenly paid salaried employee this is annual gross divided by 52. The first 6 weeks of SMP pay 90% of AWE with no cap. The next 33 weeks pay the lower of 90% AWE or the 2026/27 statutory rate of 194.32 per week.

  • Income tax is applied weekly using the personal allowance of 12,570 per year, then 20% basic to 50,270 and 40% higher to 125,140.
  • NI Class 1 employee is 0% to 242 per week, 8% to 967 per week, 2% above.
  • Salary sacrifice in place during the relevant period reduces AWE, which can reduce both the 90% phase and (rarely) the flat-rate phase.
  • A pay rise during the relevant period must be reflected in AWE under Alabaster v Woolwich (C-147/02), implemented in UK law by SI 2005/729.
