Statutory Maternity Pay, 2026/27, week by week.

Edited by Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Founder of Digital Signet. Last reviewed 23 June 2026.

SMP runs for 39 weeks. The first 6 weeks pay 90% of your average weekly earnings. The next 33 weeks pay the lower of 194.32 per week or 90% of your AWE. Enter three numbers below to see the exact schedule that will land in your bank account.

Direct answer

How much SMP do I get in 2026/27?

194.32per week, weeks 7 to 39

You get 90% of your average weekly earnings for the first 6 weeks of maternity leave, then the lower of 194.32 per week or 90% of your AWE for the next 33 weeks. SMP is taxable and subject to NI. Most employers reclaim 92% of what they pay you, or 109% (2026/27) if they qualify for Small Employers Relief. Rates are set by the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2026 (SI 2026/148) and apply from 6 April 2026.

Pregnancy weeks 12 to 25

MATB1, eligibility, notice

The MATB1 is issued by your midwife or GP from 20 weeks before EWC. You must give your employer 28 days notice in writing, no later than the end of the qualifying week.

Map the qualifying week

Leave weeks 1 to 6

The 90% phase

Your first 6 weeks pay 90% of AWE with no upper cap. A pay rise during the relevant period must be reflected in this figure under the Alabaster ruling.

Read the Alabaster rule

Leave weeks 7 to 39

The 194.32 cliff

From week 7, SMP drops to the flat statutory rate or 90% of AWE, whichever is lower. We explain the cliff with worked numbers and what enhanced employer schemes typically pay.
